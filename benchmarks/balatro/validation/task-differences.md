# Task provenance and independence

These are project-authored repair exercises with deliberately constructed
defects. They are not claims of historical upstream bugs. No Palworld task,
implementation, private assertion or game asset was copied. The Easy task
semantics were retained from the earlier Balatro candidate release; seven
versioned tasks were upgraded for v0.2. The accepted source review reasons are
summarized below. Distinct file counts or names are not the independence test.

### E1 — growth-v2

Unchanged Easy: Preserve round-end per-blind growth. Existing physical behavior and private suite are retained.

### E2 — reversible-hand-limit-v1

Unchanged Easy: Preserve per-instance owned hand-limit contribution. Existing physical behavior and private suite are retained.

### E3 — seeded-effect-stream-v1

Unchanged Easy: Preserve dedicated stream per discard. Existing physical behavior and private suite are retained.

### M1 — bounded-generator-v2

Per-instance quantities bind admission, native self-removal, captured deferred jobs, reservation release and product identity; distinct from the other logical slots.

### M2 — deck-removal-consistency-v2

Exact mixed physical target selection must agree with destruction, persistent registry/counts, a later independent cut and native draw reachability.

### M3 — live-joker-adjacency-v2

Each physical scoring contribution must follow current neighbors across reorder, sale, acquisition and later blind, without position/result sharing.

### M4 — isolated-card-wear-v2

Physical-instance wear must reset coherently on native replacement, retain final reward, isolate simultaneous cards and preserve future defaults.

### H1 — durable-charge-v2

Per-physical multi-instance receipt ledger with atomic detached snapshot restore and real native score/consume continuity; direct maps versus derived journal.

### H2 — shop-price-coherence-v2

Physical rule ownership, pure compositional quotes, versions and native pre-debit transaction commits form a distinct multi-module repair; mutable-map and replayed-event implementations use different state strategies.

### H3 — pack-choice-commit-v2

Physical preview/session identity, two-choice commitment and pending settlement across native close; map strategy and replay journal share the same API.
